✨ Creative Journey

My artistic growth

A timeline of creative milestones — from first sketch to multiplayer games. Each moment represents a step in my artistic evolution.

✏️
2022
First Pencil Sketch
The beginning of my love for graphite and shading.
A simple portrait of a mountain landscape — my first real attempt at capturing light and shadow. This sketch taught me the importance of patience and observation.
🟦
2023
Started Pixel Art
Discovered the beauty of tiny squares and retro aesthetics.
Created my first pixel art character — a little wizard with a glowing staff. This opened up a whole new world of digital creativity and precision.
✂️
2024
DIY Arts
Moved from digital to physical — paper, glue, and scissors.
Made a series of paper flowers and decorative stars, exploring texture and form. Working with my hands brought a new dimension to my creativity.
🎮
2025
Built My First Web Game
Combined coding with creativity — a playable puzzle game.
"Lumen" — a light-based puzzle game that became the start of my game development journey. It showed me how art and code can create immersive experiences.
🌐
2026
Started Multiplayer Games
Collaborative and competitive games with friends around the world.
Launched "Common Mind" and "Mystery Picture" — games that bring people together. This marked a new chapter in connecting through interactive art.
