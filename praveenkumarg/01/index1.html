✨ Creative Journey

My artistic growth

A timeline of creative milestones — from first sketch to multiplayer games.

2022
First Pencil Sketch
The beginning of my love for graphite and shading.
A simple portrait of a mountain landscape — my first real attempt at capturing light and shadow.
2023
Started Pixel Art
Discovered the beauty of tiny squares and retro aesthetics.
Created my first pixel art character — a little wizard with a glowing staff.
2024
Handmade Crafts
Moved from digital to physical — paper, glue, and scissors.
Made a series of paper flowers and decorative stars, exploring texture and form.
2025
Built My First Web Game
Combined coding with creativity — a playable puzzle game.
"Lumen" — a light-based puzzle game that became the start of my game development journey.
2026
Started Multiplayer Games
Collaborative and competitive games with friends around the world.
Launched "Common Mind" and "Mystery Picture" — games that bring people together.

The hand
behind the art

✨ "Where ideas breathe"

Creating art from tiny feelings, peaceful thoughts, and a little bit of imagination — straight from Chennai 🤍.
